The Tiger, the Monkey, and the Elephant

The Tiger, the Monkey, and the Elephant is a short children's story about three animal friends who live in a big green jungle: a tiger named Tommy, a monkey named Max, and an elephant named Ellie. The story follows the friends as they rescue Max from a tall tree, and it teaches young readers that working together makes everything better.

The story is known for its simple language, its cheerful characters, and its clear lesson about friendship. It is often read aloud to young children and is a popular choice for teaching cooperation and kindness.

Plot

Background

Tommy, Max, and Ellie are best friends who love playing together every day. Each friend has a special trait: Tommy is strong, Max is silly, and Ellie is very kind.

The rescue

The main events of the story happen in this order:

  1. One sunny morning, Max climbs a tall tree to find some delicious bananas.
  2. Max gets stuck on a high branch and shouts, Help! I can't get down!
  3. Tommy tries to climb the tree, but he is too heavy.
  4. Ellie tries to reach Max with her trunk, but the branch is too high.
  5. Ellie has an idea: she stands next to the tree, and Tommy carefully climbs onto her back.
  6. Max reaches down, grabs Tommy's paw, and with one big pull, he is safe.

Ending

After the rescue, the three friends sit under the tree and share the bananas. Tommy says the job was much easier when they worked together, Max laughs that it was also much more fun, and Ellie smiles and says, That's what friends are for! From that day on, the friends always help one another.

Characters

Tommy the Tiger

Tommy is the strongest of the three friends. Even though he is strong, he learns that strength alone is not enough to solve every problem.

Max the Monkey

Max is the silly, playful member of the group. His love of bananas is what starts the adventure when he gets stuck in the tree.

Ellie the Elephant

Ellie is the kindest of the friends and the one who comes up with the plan that saves Max.

Themes

The story explores several themes that are common in children's literature:

  • Teamwork – No single friend can save Max alone, but together they succeed.
  • Friendship – The characters care about each other and celebrate together.
  • Problem solving – When the first tries fail, the friends think of a new idea.
  • Sharing – The story ends with the friends sharing the bananas.

Moral of the story

Working together makes everything better.

Reception

Readers enjoy the story for its fun animal characters and its happy ending. Parents and teachers often use it to start conversations with children about helping others and using each person's strengths as a team.
